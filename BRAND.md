# ASCEND brand

ASCEND is a rebrand of the openGym project. The product, app, website and marketing assets are
ASCEND; inherited technical and legal identifiers stay as they are.

## Name

- Product: **ASCEND**, all caps in the UI and wordmark.
- Repository and package namespace: `jrsmDev/ASCEND`.
- Upstream project: openGym, by Duarte Santos.

## Mark

The mark is the three-polygon A from `frontend/public/icon.svg`, `frontend/public/ascend-logo.png`
and the website's `icon-*.png`. Use it as the app icon, splash, PWA icon, favicon and website brand.
It is drawn with `currentColor` in SVG so it sits on obsidian in lime or on paper in ink.

## Colour

- Obsidian: `#090A09`
- Lime accent: `#C7F45A`
- Light surfaces: `#F9F9FB` / `#FFFDF7`

## Typography

- Headlines: serif.
- Body: Inter / Manrope.

## Voice

Quiet, direct and concrete. Short sentences, no hype, no AI-sounding filler. The app is a tool
used between sets, not a coach talking at the user.

## Compatibility identifiers that must not be renamed

- Android package ID: `ch.duartesantos.opengym`
- Backup folder: `Documents/openGym`
- Environment keys, backup files and update cache names
- Production domain: `opengym.ch`
- Health Connect / Apple Health sync IDs and widget group IDs
