Meet Joseph

I’m Joseph Rman Madrilejos, a fitness professional and the founder of ASCEND. I started building the training companion I wanted for myself: clear, considered, and made to keep up with the work you put in.

ASCEND is an ongoing project shaped by real training and a belief that progress should feel personal. The goal is to help people show up with intention and recognize how far they’ve come.
