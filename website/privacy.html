Your information matters

Privacy.

This policy describes the current ASCEND website, browser demo, and Android app.

Training information

ASCEND is designed to keep your workout history, plans, goals, and settings on your device. Information is sent outside your device only when you choose a feature that needs it, such as connecting a service, exporting a backup, or using the optional AI coach.

Optional connected features

  • If you connect a server or other service, information needed for that feature is sent to the service you selected. Its operator's privacy policy applies to information it receives.
  • If you configure the optional AI coach, the training context shown in the app may be sent to the provider you select to answer your request. Review the provider's privacy terms before using it.
  • If you enable Health Connect, ASCEND can write completed workouts and weigh-ins to Health Connect on your Android device.
  • Camera and photo access are used for actions you initiate, such as scanning a code or adding your own exercise media.

Website and demo

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like other hosting providers, Vercel may process technical request information, such as an IP address, browser details, and the time of a request, to deliver and protect the service. The interactive demo runs in your browser; do not enter sensitive personal information into it.

The demo may load exercise media from an asset delivery provider. That provider may process standard request information when your browser fetches the media.

The site does not intentionally use advertising cookies. Your browser settings can control cookies and local storage used by websites.

Downloads and updates

When you check for or download an Android app update, your device connects to the ASCEND download service. The service may receive standard technical request information. The app checks for an available update from Settings, and you choose whether to download and install it.

Managing your information

You can manage training information in the app. Exported backups remain under your control. Information sent to a connected service or AI provider is also subject to that provider's controls and retention practices.

Children

ASCEND is not designed for children under 13. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through a connected feature.
